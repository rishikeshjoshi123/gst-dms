- **Delivered authority and lifecycle:** force-RLS private Review item/evidence/decision tables are reachable only through secured readers and a typed resolver. Internally contradictory or invalid observations are clarification-only; candidate selection requires at least two supported competing values. Selection appends the exact rejected/accepted field decisions, one Review decision and one Activity/outbox effect in one transaction, then closes and recomputes once. Clarification appends its decision and Activity while leaving the item open at a new revision. CAS, candidate/evidence/decision fences, actor-bound idempotency and deterministic lock order prevent stale or duplicate effects.
